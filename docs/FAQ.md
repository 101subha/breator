# Frequently Asked Questions

## Is Breator an official English word?

No.

It is currently a proposed word.

---

## Why was it created?

To describe people who not only create something but continuously nurture and sustain it.

---

## Is Breator the same as Entrepreneur?

No.

An entrepreneur focuses on starting and growing ventures.

A Breator emphasizes creating, improving, and continuously breathing life into what they build.

---

## Can anyone use this word?

Yes.

The concept is open for discussion and community feedback.
