# Breator — Official Definition (v1.0)

## Word

**Breator** *(noun)*

## Definition

A Breator is someone who creates something valuable and continuously improves, nurtures, and sustains it over time.

A Breator doesn't just start something—they stay committed to helping it grow and create lasting value.

## Word Origin

**Breath + Creator = Breator**

- Breath → Life, energy, continuity
- Creator → Building something new

Together they represent a person who creates and continuously breathes life into what they build.

## Motto

> Create. Breathe. Sustain.
