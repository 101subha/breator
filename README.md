# Breator

> **A proposed English word for people who create and continuously breathe life into what they build.**

![Version](https://img.shields.io/badge/version-v1.0-blue)
![Status](https://img.shields.io/badge/status-Proposed-success)

---

## What is Breator?

**Breator (noun)**

A **Breator** is someone who creates something valuable and continues to improve, nurture, and sustain it over time.

Unlike someone who simply starts a business or launches an idea, a Breator remains committed to helping it grow and create lasting value.

---

## Why was this word created?

Words like **Creator**, **Founder**, and **Entrepreneur** describe different parts of the journey.

**Breator** is a proposed word intended to describe the person who not only creates but also continuously breathes life into what they build.

---

## Word Origin

**Breath + Creator = Breator**

- **Breath** → Life, energy, continuity
- **Creator** → Building something new

Together they represent continuous creation and long-term commitment.
